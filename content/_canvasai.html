Four steps from holding back to holding the room.

Twelve weeks. One session a week. Every step builds on the one before.

  1. 1

    Hear yourself

    We record you in a real meeting and a real talk, then play it back. You see the pauses, filler and posture you never noticed.

  2. 2

    Take your space

    Breath, stance and pace. You learn to arrive in a room, stay still and let a sentence land.

  3. 3

    Shape the message

    One idea per talk, built around what your listener must decide. Slides become support, not a script.

  4. 4

    Perform under pressure

    Board questions, hostile Q&A, a live stage. We rehearse them until they feel familiar.
